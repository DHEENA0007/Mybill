Billing & Inventory Management Application
System Draft (Django + React + Tailwind v4)
1. Overview

The Billing & Inventory Management Application is designed to help business owners manage their stock, purchases, sales, credits, returns, and financial tracking through a single platform. The system allows complete control over inventory operations while maintaining proper records of purchase payments, sales credits, and pending balances.

Unlike traditional billing systems that rely on predefined roles such as manager, cashier, or accountant, this application introduces a dynamic role and permission management system. The owner of the business (Super Admin) can create roles dynamically and assign permissions according to the operational needs of their company.

The application ensures transparency and traceability for all operations including stock movement, purchase transactions, sales billing, credit logs, and payment settlements.

The system will be accessible across web, mobile browsers, and desktop environments through a responsive React frontend.

3. User Roles and Access Control
3.1 Super Admin

The Super Admin is the owner of the application. This is the only predefined role in the system.

Responsibilities:

Create and manage users

Create custom roles

Assign permissions

View financial reports

Configure application settings

Monitor all transactions

The Super Admin has complete system access.

Instead of hardcoded roles, the system allows the owner to create roles dynamically.
